# BosaTek Eats: disclaimers (draft, not reviewed by a lawyer)

The app shows these in three places: **About** (all of them), **onboarding** (short versions of 1 and 2,
acknowledged once), and **inline** (1 next to every allergen filter and menu item, 2 next to calorie targets).

## 1. Allergens and nutrition
**Short (inline):** Allergen info comes from ISU Dining and may be incomplete or out of date. Always confirm with dining staff.

**Full:**
Allergen, ingredient, diet and nutrition information is copied from ISU Dining's public menus and may be
incomplete, out of date, or wrong. Recipes, suppliers and portions change, and cross-contact can happen
in any kitchen. Items marked "allergen info unavailable" have no data from the source and are not
allergen-free. This app cannot guarantee that any item is free of any allergen or suitable for any diet
(including vegan, vegetarian or halal). If you have a food allergy or a medical or religious dietary
requirement, confirm every item with dining staff before eating it, and contact ISU Dining's
dietitian for accommodations.
ISU Dining's own guidance is to check the ingredient label next to each dish and ask staff, who are
happy to answer questions (dining.iastate.edu/nutrition).

## 2. Not medical advice
**Short (inline):** Calorie and macro targets are rough estimates, not medical or nutrition advice.

**Full:**
Calorie and macro targets are general estimates (Mifflin-St Jeor equation x activity level, adjusted by
goal) and are not medical, nutritional or dietary advice. They are not suitable for people who are
pregnant, under 18, managing a medical condition, or with a history of disordered eating. Talk to a
doctor or registered dietitian before changing how you eat. ISU Dining offers free dietitian consultations
to meal plan holders.

## 3. Unofficial project
BosaTek Eats is an independent student project by BosaTek. It is **not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Iowa State
University or ISU Dining**, or by Panda Express, Chick-fil-A or Sushi Do. All names and trademarks belong
to their owners. Menus, hours, payment rules, plan terms and prices can change without notice and may be
out of date here; check dining.iastate.edu and your ISU Card account for the official information.

## 4. Data source
Menu, hours and location data are read from ISU Dining's public website on a fixed schedule (no more than
about 50 requests a week, rate-limited, with an identifying User-Agent and contact address). No login or
private data is accessed. Memorial Union tenant hours are entered by hand. ISU Dining can ask for any of this
to stop at hello@bosatek.com, and it will.

## 5. Privacy
Everything you enter stays on your device (browser storage). Your location is used only to sort nearby
venues and is never stored or sent anywhere. No accounts, analytics or tracking.
